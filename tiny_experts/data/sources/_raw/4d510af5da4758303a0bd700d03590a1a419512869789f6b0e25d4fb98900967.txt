[p.61] general relativity versus quantum theory in quantum field theory. In short, quantum theory only works because it assumes that gravity does not exist. Indeed, the gravitational constant 𝐺does not appear in quantum field theory. Quantum field theory ignores 𝐺. On the other hand, general relativity neglects the commutation rules between physical quantities discovered in experiments on a microscopic scale. The concept of measurement also differs in the two approaches. In quantum mechanics, on the other hand, the precision of measurement is limited. The indeterminacy relation yields limits that follow from the mass 𝑀of the measurement apparatus.
