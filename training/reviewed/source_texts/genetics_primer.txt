CRISPR-Cas9 is an adaptive bacterial immune endonuclease system guided by single guide RNA to cleave targeted double-stranded DNA sequences adjacent to a protospacer adjacent motif (PAM). RNA polymerase II transcribes protein-coding genes into messenger RNA precursors that undergo 5-prime capping, polyadenylation, and spliceosomal intron excision. Point mutations include missense substitutions, nonsense stop codons, and insertions or deletions that shift the translational reading frame.
